Privacy Policy

How I Manage · Last updated November 18, 2026

How I Manage is a task and calendar app for Mac, iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

No server, no tracking

How I Manage has no server of its own. The developer does not collect, receive, store or see your data. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your tasks and notes

Tasks, lists and notes are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they sync between your own devices through your private iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect a Google account, the app asks Google for permission to:

  • see the list of your calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly);
  • see, create, change and delete events on your calendars (calendar.events);
  • see your email address, to show which account is connected (openid, email).

The app uses this access only to show your events and to make the changes you ask for. Your device talks directly to Google. Your Google sign-in is kept in your device's Keychain, and event data is kept only in the app's memory and on your device.

Google data is never sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. No person reads it.

How I Manage's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services you connect

If you connect other accounts, such as Apple Calendar or Notion, the app reads and writes the data you choose directly with that service on your device. Nothing passes through the developer.

Removing access and data

You can disconnect a Google account in the app's Settings, which deletes its sign-in from your device. You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes its data from that device; data in iCloud can be removed in your device's iCloud settings.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions can be raised at github.com/JacoverLaw/jacoverlaw.github.io/issues.
